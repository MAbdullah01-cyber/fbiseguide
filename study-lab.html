Recommended Study Flow

  • Start with the syllabus and chapter-wise SLOs.
  • Learn the concept, then practise MCQs and descriptive questions.
  • Use the official FBISE model paper to understand the paper structure.
  • Attempt a timed mock test and review mistakes.
  • Repeat weak chapters and use the 30-day planner for final revision.
