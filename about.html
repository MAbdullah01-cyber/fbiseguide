About Federal Board Guide

Federal Board Guide is an independent educational resource designed to organize useful FBISE study information in a simple student-friendly structure.

What the site provides

  • Class-wise navigation for Grade 9, Grade 10, 1st Year and 2nd Year.
  • Links to official FBISE model papers and assessment frameworks.
  • Old-paper, syllabus and practical-resource shortcuts.
  • Study preparation guidance, SLO-focused practice ideas and exam planning.
  • Student services such as roll slips, results and latest news links.

Official-source policy

Where a document or service belongs to FBISE, the site sends students to the official FBISE website or portal. The site does not claim to be FBISE and does not imply endorsement.

Disclaimer: Verify current examination information, deadlines, fees, results and notices on the official FBISE sources before relying on them.
